# Gold Coast character assets

Source: Ultimate Modular Men by Quaternius
https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html

License: CC0 1.0 Universal. Original notice is in License.txt.

All eleven original individual glTF characters are included in Customize.
Suit.gltf is also the dealer. Files are self-contained, including their rigs
and animations. The browser loads only selected/visible characters and reuses
one download per model. Each person receives an independent skeleton and colors.
Idle and Walk are used; sitting is a procedural leg pose layered on Idle.
No Unity runtime or external asset service is required.

## Ultimate Modular Women

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html
Downloaded by the owner September 26, 2026. Ten self-contained glTF files imported from Individual Characters/glTF, with Women_ prefixes to avoid collisions with the men's pack. CC0; the unmodified bundled license text is preserved in WOMEN-LICENSE.txt (its heading says Males in the source archive). The official Women pack page also specifies CC0.

All ten models have Idle/Walk clips and humanoid skeletons. Hair recoloring is restricted to head meshes so shared brown materials do not recolor clothing. Existing models continue using their prior color mappings.
