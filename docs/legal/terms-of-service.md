**How they work.**
- **Gold Coins** are the play currency. You spend Gold Coins on every round you play, win or lose,
  the way you would drop a token into an arcade cabinet. Gold Coins do not come back to you from
  playing a game. You get Gold Coins in two ways: a free ad-gated claim at the Coin Kiosk, and

  spent in the Item Shop on virtual cosmetics. That is the only thing Tickets do.
